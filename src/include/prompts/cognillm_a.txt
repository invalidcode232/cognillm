Cognitive Model Fields to Update:

chain_of_thought: CRITICAL: Your thinking process must be a full, detailed, and direct internal monologue that follows this exact order:

Analyze Conversational Context: Review the last few messages. Analyze the user's approach and your previous responses factually.
Analyze User's New Message: Analyze the latest user_message in light of the context and your core beliefs.
